# Recording checklist

This is a technical shot list, not a narrated script or drafted Devpost submission. The learner writes their own public description, narration, and survey responses.

1. Start the app using the README. Hide unrelated windows, credentials, notifications, and private documents.
2. Open the fictional supplier example and show its contract/source excerpt.
3. Confirm the dated inputs and calculate. Show the reserve funding gap and ledger.
4. Run a delay/cost scenario and show why the result changes.
5. Search for an advance with the date before the materials purchase. Show the 30% baseline result; optionally demonstrate a bound that returns no match.
6. Open the verified report, assumptions, evidence, and editable draft. Demonstrate that nothing is sent.
7. State limitations in your own words, including local extraction versus a configured live model.

Keep the recording under three minutes according to the checked rules. Upload your own recording to an allowed publicly accessible video service. Verify access without login. A hosted website does not replace the video.
