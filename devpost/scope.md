# TermWise AI

Payment-term decision support for a small supplier deciding whether a new order is fundable.

## The Unique Kernel
Calculate feasible advances and milestone schedules from a dated cash-flow ledger and a user-defined reserve constraint. Every option is verified with the same engine used for the original deal.

## Who It's For
A freelancer, small agency, or supplier who pays delivery expenses before receiving customer payment. The first example is a fictional INR 200,000 supplier order.

## The Core Loop
Upload or enter terms, inspect evidence, confirm amounts and dates, enter costs and available cash, calculate the timeline, stress assumptions, search for alternatives, and review a report and negotiation draft.

## Inspiration & Identity
The user-supplied TermWise AI build brief defines a calm, coherent fintech workspace with readable charts, transparent assumptions, restrained animation, and no arbitrary risk scores. Exact palette is an implementation choice: cream, forest green, and muted lime; no gradients. No existing app was cloned.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The user selected the idea for Build With AI: Basics and supplied a detailed implementation brief. Further personal motivation and learning objectives have not been established.

## What "Working" Looks Like
The fictional supplier example shows INR -40,000 minimum cash, INR 60,000 funding needed for a INR 20,000 reserve, and a verified 30% advance when received before expenses. Editing an input invalidates results. A reduced maximum advance honestly returns no feasible candidate within bounds.

## The POC Boundary
One deal, one currency at a time, a dated event ledger, editable source evidence, PDF/DOCX/text input, conditional scenarios, bounded advance search with configurable dates and optional milestone, and an exportable decision record. No live model is required for deterministic calculations.

## Later
OCR, saved cases, broader benchmarks, richer optimization, and live-provider quality evaluation.

## Explicitly Cut
Bank connections, payments, lending, customer credit prediction, autonomous messaging, multi-user accounts, and enterprise storage are excluded because they do not establish the kernel.
